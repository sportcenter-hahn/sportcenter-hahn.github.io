Frequently asked questions

Before you commit

How do I book a court?

Online only — via the booking page or the Eversports app: pick a venue, pick a date and time, done. You see live which courts are free. We do not take reservations by phone, so that everyone sees the same availability.

Can I play without being a member?

Yes. Either book a court as a private visitor, or play as a member's guest and pay the guest fee. Both are straightforward.

How do I become a member?

Through the application form on the membership page. We will then get in touch with the next steps.

Can I play at both venues?

Yes. The combined membership covers both Geretsried and Wolfratshausen.

Are there leagues and tournaments?

Regularly. In padel we run our own Bavaro Padel League, plus tournaments and team fixtures — you will find an overview on the leagues page.

Can I bring friends?

Of course. Just register them as guests.

Do I have to pay in advance?

Yes. The online booking system asks for payment at the time of booking.

How do I reach a person?

By phone on 08171 51 110, by email to mail@sportcenter-hahn.de or via WhatsApp. All the options are listed on the contact page.
