Golf simulator

Play golf all year round — in January, in the rain, and after work.

Geretsried

Golf simulator at STC Oberland

The simulator measures the club head and the ball flight and calculates the trajectory from them. You see immediately what face, angle and speed are doing — feedback you simply do not get on a driving range.

It gets used in two ways: as winter practice so the season does not start from zero, and as a round with friends in the evening.

  • Independent of weather and course season
  • Up to four people per booking
  • Instant analysis of every shot
  • Suitable for beginners without a course permit

Do I need a course permit?

No. Anyone can start on the simulator — it is in fact the more forgiving place to do so than a driving range.

How long does a round take?

For 18 holes with two people, allow a good two hours; with four, correspondingly more. Pure technique practice also works in an hour.

Court free?

Availability in real time, booking in under a minute. Open daily from 7 am to 11 pm.
