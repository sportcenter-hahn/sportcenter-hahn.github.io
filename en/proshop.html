Hire

Turning up with nothing works too

  • For a first try

    Nobody needs to buy anything to try padel or pickleball. Hire rackets and balls for the hour.

  • For guests

    Brought someone who does not play? Two hire rackets, and the spectator becomes a player.

  • For emergencies

    Broken string, forgotten racket, dead balls. It happens — and it is sorted here in two minutes.

Stringing

A new racket is rarely the answer

A tired string bed costs you more control than any frame can give back. The rule of thumb: restring as many times a year as you play per week. Playing twice a week means roughly every six months.

We string on site — with string from our range or your own. Tension as you like it; if you are not sure, we work it out from how you play.
