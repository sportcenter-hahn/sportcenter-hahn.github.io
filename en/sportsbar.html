Sports bar

The bar at the Geretsried venue — meeting point after the match, stage for home fixtures and tournaments.

Geretsried

Where an hour turns into an evening

The Geretsried venue has its own sports bar. It is the reason teams stay seated after the match, why tournament days do not end with the prize-giving and why our home fixtures have an atmosphere you will not find at other clubs.

What goes on here

More than a counter

  • Home fixtures

    Bundesliga match days in Geretsried become an event here. Cowbells expressly permitted — a habit we have picked up over the years.

  • Tournaments & prize-givings

    STC Series, STC Gaudi, junior tournaments: the day does not end on court, it ends here.

  • Celebrations

    The carnival party once a year, and in between birthdays, company evenings and team dinners. [[ FROM WHAT GROUP SIZE? ]]
