Focus areas

What we work on with you

  • Serve

    Powerful, accurate serves so every point starts on your terms.

    • Clean technique
    • Better placement
    • More confidence on court
    • Consistent build-up

  • Strategy & tactics

    Read opponents, make better decisions, win points with your head.

    • Tactical awareness
    • Smarter shot selection
    • Controlled movement
    • Stronger under pressure

  • Doubles

    Communication, positioning and timing — so you win more matches.

    • Team communication
    • Better court coverage
    • Coordinated play
    • Practice under match pressure

  • Footwork

    Sharpen agility and running technique so you are always in the right position and reach more balls.

    • Agility training
    • Optimal positioning
    • Balance & stability
    • Efficient movement patterns
