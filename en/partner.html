Find a partner

Who is looking for a game right now? Post your request or say yes to an open one — no sign-up.

    Post a request

    This is visible to every visitor of this site — otherwise nobody can respond. Use a number or address you are happy to share for this.

    Your entry disappears by itself once the time slot has passed. You can withdraw it yourself at any point.
    Join

    That is all we need. You will then see how to reach whoever posted it.

    How it works

    Three steps, no sign-up

    • Post it

      Sport, day, time slot, how many are missing. Plus your first name and a way to reach you. Half a minute.

    • Join

      Anyone who wants to play clicks “Count me in” and leaves only a first name. They then see how to reach you.

    • Book a court

      You sort the rest out between you — and one of you books the court through the booking system.

    The board is not a reservation: it brings you together, you book the court as usual.

    Worth knowing

    What happens to your details

    Who can see my contact details?

    Every visitor to this site — the form says so too. Without a way to reach you nobody can respond, which is why this is the only public detail. Use a number or address you are happy to share for this. People who join, by contrast, give only a first name.

    How long does my entry stay up?

    Until the time slot has passed, then it disappears on its own. You can withdraw it any time — the button appears on your own entry as long as you are in the same browser.

    Do I need an account?

    No. A first name is enough for now. If the board catches on we will add a member login on top of it — contact details would then stay among members.

    Do I have to be a member?

    No, the board is open to everyone. As a member you then book the court cheaper and earlier — see Membership.

    Found a partner?

    Then all that is missing is the court.
